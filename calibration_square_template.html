📐 30mm Calibration Square - Print Template

⚠️ Important: For accurate measurements, printer settings must be correct!

Printing Instructions:

  1. Check Printer Settings
    • Paper size: A4 or Letter
    • Scale: 100% (No scaling, no "Fit to page")
    • Orientation: Portrait
    • Print quality: High/Best
  2. Print this page - Click the "Print" button below
  3. Verify size - Measure the printed square with a ruler
    • Should be exactly 30mm × 30mm
    • If not, adjust printer scale settings and reprint
  4. Cut out carefully - For best results, cut along the outer border
  5. Optional: Laminate - Protect the square by laminating it

Tips for Best Results:

  • Use thick paper or cardstock for durability
  • Ensure printer is set to "Actual Size" not "Fit to page"
  • Measure with a metric ruler to verify accuracy
  • Keep the square flat when using with the camera
  • Mount on cardboard or foam board for rigidity
